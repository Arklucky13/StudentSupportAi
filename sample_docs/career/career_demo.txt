
DEMO DOCUMENT — GENERAL CAREER GUIDANCE

Topic: Internship Preparation

Students preparing for internships can create a one-page resume
highlighting relevant skills, academic projects, internships,
certifications, and links to their portfolio or GitHub.

Before applying, students should review the job description,
check the eligibility criteria, and tailor their application to
the role.

An internship application may request a resume, portfolio,
academic details, or a college permission letter. Requirements
vary by employer, so students should verify the specific listing.
